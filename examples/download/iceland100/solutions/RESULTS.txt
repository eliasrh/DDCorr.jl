BÁRÐARBUNGA · THE REFERENCE REPORT

Open report.html inside Iceland100/reference. The example uses 3,300 SAC
files from 100 earthquakes recorded on three components at 11 stations.

DDCorr accepted 2,412 P measurements and 4,625 S measurements. Each of
the 7,037 measurements compares two events at one station for one phase.
Several stations and both phases can measure the same pair. Together the
accepted measurements connect 1,914 distinct event pairs.

In a waveform example, the first panel shows the two records relative to
their P or S markers. The aligned traces show how much their waveforms
agree after applying the measured time shift. The lag curve gives the
correlation coefficient for each shift considered.

The fitted-weight run flags 61 observations at |r| >= 0.99, including
all seven for events 85 and 86. coefficient_audit.csv gives the sampled
and fitted lags and their coefficients.

s_horizontal_components.csv lists each horizontal separately for the
3,973 accepted S observations that used two components. own_grid_lag_ms
and own_peak_r describe that channel's strongest sampled peak. The joint
columns describe the common grid or fitted lag selected for both channels.
r_at_joint_grid and r_at_joint_fit give the contribution of this channel
at each of those two lags. The sampled mean must reach 0.70. DDCorr writes
the square of the fitted mean as the weight. All lags in this table are
milliseconds relative to the event picks. Positive means the feature in
event j is later on its pick-centred axis.

The horizontal maxima occupy the same grid point for 3,598 pairs of
records. In 375 they differ by one 20 ms grid interval. For 736 of the
3,973 observations, one component is below 0.70 at the shared grid lag.
